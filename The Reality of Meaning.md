# The Reality of Meaning

This logic builds on Peirce’s Triadic Semiotics and Rovelli’s contention that the world is fundamentally made of relationships (triads) rather than substances.

1. Reality is composed of relations (triads). (Rovelli, Peirce)

2. Only stable triads last, endure.
   - (i.e., when a triad becomes unstable it goes out of existence)
   - Note that this applies to animals, tornados, companies, economies, ecologies etc.

3. Natural selection is just a description of the stability criteria acting at the level of ecologies.

4. The affording, specifying, and satisfying partitioning is simply a framework for thinking about relations within the triad.

5. Satisfying, however, has a privileged status
   - (top of the Abstraction Hierarchy, because it specifies the criteria for existence. Why one thing exists and another becomes extinct.)
   - Satisfying determines the ‘goodness’ or ‘fitness’ of a thing.

6. Affording, and specifying determine the potential means (how) for achieving satisfying (meeting the criteria for existence).
   - Affording reflects the energy coupling within the triad. (Forward portion of the loop, acting)
   - Specifying reflects the information coupling within the triad. (Feedback portion of the loop, perceiving)
   - Together they create the condition (closed loop) where energy can be directed toward achieving stability.

7. Affording and Specifying are explicit properties of the coupling (the control system), but satisfying is implicit - there is no box for it.
   - The satisfying criteria can be internal to the loop (conditions for survival - self organizing system) or external (set by an external designer)
